Inky & Ego · Two in One

Inky and Ego are one being held together by Ego — an invisible connective energy only they can feel. Together they are one. Split they are two fully distinct people who look nothing alike.

Abilities
Split
Original must consent and release. Once split they look nothing alike and operate independently.
Rejoin
Either can return into Ego energy at will. Both can phase through each other selectively by choice.
Ego Link
While split, all memory and knowledge passes instantly between them. Everything except opinion.
Weightless
To each other: no physical weight or resistance. Contact bypasses all force.
Detachment
Either can release limbs to the other's control — fully, immediately, no lag. Detached limbs have 1HP. One hit returns them with no consequence. Cooldown is DM discretion. Damage still dealt to owner.
Resurrection
As long as Ego energy remains, the survivor can re-split the dead one back at 1HP. Not revival — splitting again. Three times per full rest.
HP Rules
Together — full shared pool.
Split — divides evenly. Inky gets the odd point.
Rejoining — sum of both current pools becomes the merged HP.
After resurrection — returns at exactly 1 HP.
If both die simultaneously — permanent. No resurrection possible.
